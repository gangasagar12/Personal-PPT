Ganga Sagar Joshi
Django Backend Developer • Kathmandu, Nepal

Hey! I'm Ganga Sagar Joshi, a software developer and Django backend engineer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I hold a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) from Kantipur City College, affiliated with Purbanchal University.

I specialize in architecting scalable server-side systems, high-throughput REST APIs, and normalized relational database schemas with Python, Django, PostgreSQL, and MySQL. My engineering approach centers on writing clean, maintainable code and solving real-world institutional problems.

As Co-Founder and technical lead at Inovix Technologies, I've built production clinical practice platforms (such as Oralix and CareFirst Dental), unifying multi-chair operatory scheduling, patient health records, and billing.

When I'm not writing backend services or optimizing database queries, you'll find me exploring AWS cloud infrastructure, containerized deployments with Docker, and practical machine learning workflows.

I believe software engineering is the most effective medium to create tangible value for businesses and communities. Whether collaborating with startups, enterprise teams, or institutional clients, I prioritize clear architecture and intuitive user experiences.

Outside of tech, I love traveling, exploring new perspectives, and learning continuously to grow personally and professionally. If you have an exciting project or want to collaborate, let's create something awesome together!
