Oralix — Dental Practice OS

Client: Niko Dental & Aesthetics
Official Site: nikodentistry.com

Project Introduction

Niko Dental & Aesthetics is a dental healthcare provider with clinics in Koteshwor, Kathmandu, and Campus Chowk, Dhangadi, offering a wide range of dental services, including general dentistry, dental implants, root canal treatment, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, and preventive dental care. Strataflow Technologies Pvt. Ltd. developed a modern, responsive website for the clinic to strengthen its online presence and make important information easily accessible to patients. The platform brings together dental services, specialist profiles, treatment pricing, clinic locations, facilities, patient testimonials, and educational resources. It also includes online appointment requests, a dental video section, and an AI-powered dental assistant, providing visitors with a convenient way to explore treatments, find clinic information, and connect with the healthcare team.

Core Features & System Capabilities

  • Real-time operatory chair scheduling and interactive patient appointment booking
  • Electronic Health Records (EHR) with digital dental charting and diagnosis records
  • Automated invoice calculation, transparent fee schedules, and multi-tier billing
  • Interactive AI-powered dental assistant providing instant treatment guidance and FAQs
  • Multi-branch clinical routing between Kathmandu and Dhangadi clinic locations
  • Stateless RESTful APIs powering mobile patient check-in workflows

Business Impact & Results

Established a comprehensive digital platform for Niko Dental & Aesthetics, bringing its dental services, specialist information, treatment pricing, clinic locations, and patient resources together in one place. The website makes it easier for patients to explore available treatments, review clinic information, request appointments, and access dental health education. Its interactive features also provide additional ways for visitors to find information and connect with the clinic.
